# Edit Department

> **Module:** `ssi_hr`\
> **Model:** `hr.department`\
> **Menu:** Human Resource > Configuration > Organization > Departments\
> **Actor:** user in group _Department_\
> **Requires:** `01-create`

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** The department is active.
- **Access:** User is in group _Department_.

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Configuration > Organization > Departments** menu.
2. Find and open the record to edit.
3. Click the **Edit** button.
4. Change the required fields, for example **Department Name**, **Manager**, or **Parent
   Department**.
5. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The record is updated with the new values.
