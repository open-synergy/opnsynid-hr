# Delete Employee

> **Module:** `ssi_hr`\
> **Model:** `hr.employee`\
> **Menu:** Human Resource > Employees\
> **Actor:** user in group _Employee_\
> **Requires:** `01-create`

## Pre-Condition

- **Access:** User is in group _Employee_.

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Employees** menu.
2. Find and open the record to delete.
3. Click the **Action** menu, then click **Delete**.
4. Click **OK** on the confirmation dialog.

## Post-Condition

- The record is removed from the Employees list.
