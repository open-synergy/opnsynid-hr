# Delete Job Position

> **Module:** `ssi_hr`\
> **Model:** `hr.job`\
> **Menu:** Human Resource > Configuration > Organization > Job Positions\
> **Actor:** user in group _Job Position_\
> **Requires:** `01-create`

## Pre-Condition

- **Access:** User is in group _Job Position_.

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Configuration > Organization > Job Positions** menu.
2. Find and open the record to delete.
3. Click the **Action** menu, then click **Delete**.
4. Click **OK** on the confirmation dialog.

## Post-Condition

- The record is removed from the Job Positions list.
